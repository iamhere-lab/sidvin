Our Story

Where Time and Space Began to Matter Again

Over the years, something quietly changed in our cities. Time, which once moved with us, began to move ahead of us. Commutes grew longer. Distances shaped our days. Moments meant for family, for pause, for ourselves — slowly disappeared.

At the same time, homes began changing too. They were no longer shaped by living, but by numbers. Rooms became efficient. Proportions tightened. Space adjusted, adapted, and slowly felt less our own.

And somewhere along the way, the feeling of having one's own space and pace faded.

When we decided to build Sidvin Celeste, we asked ourselves a simple question:

What if a home could give time and space back — instead of taking them away?
Not as luxury. Not as excess. But as something essential to family life.

We believed space is something families never regret choosing. That privacy becomes more valuable with time, and that a home should feel calm — not just on the first day, but for years to come.

Families evolve, Children grow, Parents age. Lives expand in ways no floor plan can predict.

Sidvin Celeste is designed to support those changes — without compromise.
This is not a home you move on from, This is a home you settle into.

Project Overview

An Address That Rises Above Convention

Sidvin Celeste is a low-density residential development located at HAL · Old Airport Road (beside NPS Vibhuthipura). Designed for long-term living, it offers only 110 residences, ensuring privacy, space, and a calm living environment.

Homes are available in spacious configurations: 2 BHK (1554 sq. ft.), 3 BHK (2038 sq. ft.), and 4 BHK (2567 sq. ft.), each thoughtfully planned with three balconies, zero common walls, and 10-ft high ceilings for enhanced openness and natural light.

All layouts are Vaastu-aligned, combining functionality with harmony. Residents also enjoy rooftop amenities and lifetime open views, creating a lifestyle that balances comfort, design, and everyday ease.

A home built for space, privacy, and lasting value.
